Join us on September 14 for the [2026 Workshop on Urban Resilience: Challenges, Solutions and Opportunities](https://lnkd.in/eeP7_h3f), co-organized by The New York Climate Exchange and the Georgia Tech Brook Byers Institute for Sustainable Systems.

The discussion brings together researchers and practitioners to explore how cities can adapt, recover, and thrive in the face of extreme weather and other disruptions, with a focus on AI, sustainable infrastructure, and community resilience.

Organized with Xiao Liu, Allen Hyde, Jung-Ho Lewe, Patrick Kastner, Jennifer Hirsch, the Georgia Tech Center for Sustainable Communities Research and Education (SCoRE), and Georgia Institute of Technology.
